# Excel Sales and Finance Reports

## Project Overview
Built interactive Sales and Finance Reports in Excel to evaluate sales performance, customer contribution, and financial results using real-world business data.

## Sales Analysis
- Analyzed customer-wise net sales performance.
- Compared actual sales against sales targets.
- Evaluated sales trends across markets and fiscal years.
- Identified key customers and areas of underperformance.

## Finance Analysis
- Created Profit and Loss (P&L) reports by year, month, and market.
- Analyzed net sales, costs, and profitability.
- Compared financial performance across different reporting periods.

## Tools and Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query for data cleaning and transformation
- Power Pivot for data modeling
- DAX for calculated measures and KPIs
- PivotTables and charts for reporting

## Key Learnings
- Improved skills in business data analysis and reporting.
- Practiced building data models and writing DAX measures.
- Learned to translate business data into actionable insights.
